# GTM.md

## Go-To-Market: Leakproof as a Customer 
Acquisition Wedge for Credex

---

## Who We Are Actually Targeting

Not "startups." That is too vague to act on.

The exact person is an **Engineering Manager 
or CTO at a Series A startup — 15 to 80 
people** — already spending $500-$5,000 a 
month on AI tools with no visibility into 
whether that spend makes sense.

These teams moved fast. Engineers expensed 
tools individually. Now they have Cursor, 
Copilot, ChatGPT Team, Claude Team, and API 
credits running simultaneously and nobody 
knows what is actually being used.

The pain is not "AI is too expensive." 
The pain is: "We moved fast and now our 
tooling spend is a mess and finance is 
asking questions."

One thing worth noting: the person who 
feels this most acutely is often the CFO 
or Head of Finance — not the engineering 
manager. They see a $3,000 Anthropic API 
bill with no context for whether it is 
reasonable. Finance communities are an 
underexplored channel for this reason.

---

## What They Are Doing The Week Before

Usually a budget review triggers it. 
They search:

 "Cursor vs Copilot worth it for small teams"
 "ChatGPT Team plan is it worth it"
 "reduce AI tooling spend startup"

They read The Pragmatic Engineer, Lenny's 
Newsletter, Ben's Bites, TLDR AI. They 
scan Hacker News daily.

---

## Where They Hang Out

r/ExperiencedDevs is best subreddit — 
senior, technical, people actively discussing 
tooling decisions. startups is noisier 
but has the right job titles.

Lenny's Newsletter Slack is the highest 
signal community. The people there are 
operators at growth-stage companies.

On X — search "AI costs" or "Cursor pricing" 
filtered to the last 7 days. Build a private 
list of engineering leaders posting about 
tooling. The signal is better than it looks 
if you filter for founder or engineering 
titles in their bio.

The underexplored channel: finance 
communities. r/CFO and accounting Slack 
groups reach the person with budget 
authority and the most acute pain. 
Nobody else is targeting this angle.

## First 100 Users — $0 Budget

The HN launch is the real bet. 
Everything else is backup.

**Day 1-3: Show HN**

> Show HN: Free AI spend audit — finds 
> overlap and oversized plans across 
> ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot

Submit 8-10am US Eastern on a Monday 
or Tuesday. If it does not get traction 
in 6 hours it will not. Move on — 
do not resubmit.

Realistic outcome: 1000-2000 visits, 
30-50 audits, 8-15 email captures.

**Day 4-7: Cold DMs on X**

15-20 per day to founders who recently 
posted about AI tooling costs. Reference 
something specific they said — generic 
DMs get ignored.

> "Hey — saw your tweet about [specific 
> thing]. Built a free AI spend audit 
> that finds overlap across ChatGPT, 
> Claude, Cursor. Takes 2 mins."

**Day 8-20: Newsletter Pitch**

After collecting data, pitch this angle:

> "We audited 50 startup teams. Average 
> AI overspend: ~$340/month."

Target Ben's Bites, TLDR AI, The 
Pragmatic Engineer. Data is the story — 
not the tool.
