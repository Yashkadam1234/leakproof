# ECONOMICS.md

## Can a Free AI Spend Audit Make Money 
for Credex?

Yes — but the reason is more interesting 
than "free tool generates leads."

Most lead generation gives you an email 
and a guess about intent. This tool gives 
you an email, the exact dollar amount the 
prospect spends on AI tools, which tools, 
how many seats, and their primary use case.

That is not a lead. That is a qualified 
buyer profile with a known budget floor.

When someone completes an audit showing 
$3,000/month in AI spend — Credex knows 
this team has budget. Most sales teams 
spend weeks qualifying that. The audit 
surfaces it in 2 minutes for free.

## 1. What Is a Lead Worth?

These are estimates — I do not have 
Credex's actual deal data.

A 20-person startup spending seriously 
on AI pays $2,000-$8,000/year on tooling 
and credits. At 15-25% margin on 
discounted credits:
Customer spend: $5,000/year
Credex margin: 20%
Gross profit: $1,000/year
LTV at 18 months: ~$1,500-$15,000

Reverse engineering lead value:
Mid LTV: $6,000
Close rate: 15%
Consultation value: ~$900
Qualified lead value: ~$150

Even at 10% close rate a booked 
consultation is worth $600. A free 
audit generating these leads is an 
obvious trade.

The number I am least confident about 
is the 18-month lifespan. If teams 
churn after one purchase the LTV drops 
significantly and the model gets tighter.

## 2. The Viral Coefficient Nobody Counts

Every shared audit URL is free outreach 
on Credex's behalf.

When someone shares:
"See how much this team could save"
https://leakproof-gules.vercel.app/audit/xxx

The recipient wonders about their own spend.

A viral coefficient of even 0.2 — 2 in 
10 viewers run their own audit — changes 
the funnel without increasing acquisition 
cost. Most lead gen tools are not shareable 
by design. This one is.

## 3. CAC by Channel

| Channel | Cost | Leads/month | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| HN Show HN | $0 | 20-40 | $0 |
| Cold DM on X | ~$50 time | 5-15 | $3-10 |
| Newsletter mention | $0-500 | 30-80 | $0-17 |
| Paid retargeting | $500/mo | 20-40 | $12-25 |
| Credex existing customers | $0 | unknown | $0 |

The Credex existing customer row is the 
most interesting and hardest to estimate. 
CAC is zero and intent is high. This 
should be activated before any paid channel.

## 4. Funnel Math
1,000 audits/month
↓ 25% email capture
250 qualified leads
↓ 8% consultation booking
20 booked calls
↓ 15% close rate
3 new customers/month
× $5,000 average deal
= $15,000 ARR added monthly

The 8% consultation booking rate is 
the assumption I am most uncertain 
about. At 3-4% the monthly number 
drops 1-2 customers. Still positive 
unit economics but slower.

---

## 5. What Has to Be True for $1M ARR
5,000 audits/month
→ 1,250 leads
→ 100 consultations
→ 15 customers/month
× $5,000 average deal
= $75,000 new ARR/month
→ $1M ARR in ~14 months

Four things need to be true:

1. HN launch gets meaningful traction
2. Email capture rate stays above 20%
3. Credex sales team scales to handle 
   100 calls/month by month 14
4. Average deal stays around $5,000

None of these require anything 
extraordinary. But none are guaranteed.

The most important input in any unit 
economics model is whether the product 
solves a real problem for a buyer who 
already has budget.

In this case the buyer is already 
spending the money. They just do not 
know they are wasting part of it.

That is the best possible starting 
point for a sales conversation.
