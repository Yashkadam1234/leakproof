## FAQ

**1. We already have a spreadsheet tracking 
this. Why would we use this instead?**

A spreadsheet tracks what you tell it. 
This benchmarks what you are paying against 
what teams your size actually need — and 
flags when a plan is oversized for your 
seat count or when two tools are doing 
the same job.

If your spreadsheet is already doing that, 
you probably do not need this. Most are not.

**2. You sell AI credits. Are your savings 
estimates just pushing us toward Credex?**

Fair concern. The audit engine is rules-based 
— every recommendation traces to an official 
vendor pricing page with a date. We do not 
use AI to generate savings numbers because 
that would make them easy to game.

Credex only appears in the results when 
your total spend crosses a threshold where 
discounted credits genuinely make sense. 
For smaller audits it does not show up at all.

**3. What happens when vendor pricing 
changes after I run my audit?**

Pricing is verified against official vendor 
pages before each submission. Every number 
has a source URL and a verification date — 
you can check them yourself in the report.

If something looks wrong — a price changed 
since we last checked — email us. We would 
rather fix it than have stale data sitting 
in the tool quietly misleading people.

**4. Do you need access to our billing 
accounts or internal systems?**

No. We never connect to your Stripe, 
vendor dashboards, AWS billing, or anything 
else. The audit runs entirely from what 
you type in.

The tradeoff is that the recommendations 
are only as accurate as the numbers you 
enter. If you round up or guess, the 
output reflects that.

**5. What if the audit says we are 
already spending well?**

It will say exactly that. We have
specific result state for optimized stacks 
that does not manufacture savings to 
justify the tool existing.

Honestly the "you're already optimized" 
result is one of the more useful ones — 
it means you can stop second-guessing 
your AI spend and focus on something else.
